How to Use This Form

This tool provides a combined score from Audience Relevance and Sourcing Quality to give you an immediate translation priority level.

  1. Complete all three questions in the Audience Relevance Filter (Q1).
  2. Complete all checkboxes in the Sources question (Q2).
  3. Click "Evaluate Priority" at the bottom to get the result and next steps.
